<h1 id="integrity-overview">Code Integrity & Fair Play</h1>

Maintain transparency, original authorship, and ethical standards within the NGCBD showcase platform and community.

---

<h2 id="understanding-integrity">1. Understanding Code Integrity</h2>

Code integrity means taking full ownership of your technical submissions and maintaining absolute transparency regarding how your software was constructed.

<h3 id="integrity-principles">Core Integrity Principles</h3>

* **Original Authorship:** The primary logic, architecture, and implementation of submitted projects must reflect your own engineering effort.
* **Honest Attribution:** Using third-party libraries, open-source modules, or boilerplates is permitted only when properly credited in the project repository.
* **Transparent Commit History:** Submissions must demonstrate organic development history through clear and progressive GitHub commits.

---

<h2 id="plagiarism-vs-inspiration">2. Plagiarism vs. Inspiration</h2>

Drawing inspiration from existing open-source software is natural in software development, but direct, uncredited copying crosses the line into plagiarism.

<h3 id="drawing-the-line">Key Distinctions</h3>

* **Inspiration & Attribution (Allowed):** Studying an existing project's architecture, building a custom implementation from scratch, or using an open-source library while giving explicit credit in the `README.md` and source code.
* **Plagiarism & Misrepresentation (Forbidden):** Copying someone else's repository, renaming variables or styles, and presenting it as your original creation without attribution.

---

<h2 id="anti-spamming">3. Anti-Spamming Guidelines</h2>

To maintain a high-signal environment for all members, spamming behavior across the platform, GitHub, and social channels is strictly prohibited.

<h3 id="prohibited-actions">Prohibited Spam Activities</h3>

* Submitting low-effort tutorial clones, default template setups, or empty repositories to farm activity points.
* Posting non-technical, promotional, or repetitive comments on peer repositories and community forums.
* Mass-tagging members or reviewers to bypass standard evaluation queues.

---

<h2 id="detection-verification">4. Detection & Verification Process</h2>

NGCBD utilizes a multi-layered verification protocol to inspect every submitted project before confirming leaderboard scores and awarding badges.

<h3 id="verification-layers">Verification Steps</h3>

* **Automated Code Analysis:** Scanning public repositories for direct code duplication, plagiarized commit histories, and uncredited forks.
* **Manual Architectural Review:** Peer reviewers inspect code structure, dependency graphs, and custom logic depth.
* **Authenticity Challenge:** Reviewers reserve the right to request a brief technical discussion with the author to verify their understanding of the submitted codebase.

---

<h2 id="violations-transparency">5. Consequences of Violation & Transparency Notice</h2>

NGCBD maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards deliberate plagiarism and fraud to protect the integrity of honest developers.

<h3 id="enforcement-protocol">Enforcement Protocol</h3>

* **Immediate Community Ban:** Any developer proven to have plagiarized software or engaged in intentional fraud will face an immediate ban from the community and league cycles.
* **Internal Transparency Notice:** An official notice detailing the violation will be published strictly within the internal community channels to maintain community transparency and awareness.
* **Path to Reinstatement:** A banned author may appeal and re-enter the community if they can definitively prove original authorship of the flagged code or demonstrate full resolution of the attribution issue.
