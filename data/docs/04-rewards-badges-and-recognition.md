<h1 id="rewards-overview">Rewards, Badges & Recognition</h1>

Learn how NGCBD and Motion Mind celebrate developer excellence through physical rewards, digital credentials, and merit-based verified badges.

---

<h2 id="bi-monthly-rewards">1. Bi-Monthly Rewards Program</h2>

At the end of every 2-month league cycle, top-performing developers and high-signal contributors are selected for official recognition and exclusive developer swags.

<h3 id="reward-philosophy">Zero Pressure Rewards Philosophy</h3>

In line with our **Zero Pressure Philosophy**, the bi-monthly league gives developers an ample 2-month window to build meaningful software without artificial pressure or tight sprint deadlines.

* **League Champions:** Awarded the official **Motion Mind Mystery Swag Box** (featuring exclusive digital badges, physical collectibles, and limited-edition developer gear).
* **Top Performers Showcase:** Featured prominently on the NGCBD platform header, official social channels, and awarded digital Certificates of Excellence.
* **Community Reviewer Award:** Special recognition and rewards for developers who provide the most constructive code feedback and open meaningful PRs/issues on peer repositories.

---

<h2 id="verified-badges">2. Verified Badges</h2>

**Verified Badges** are merit-based digital achievements awarded to developers who demonstrate genuine technical depth and clean engineering practices in their project submissions.

<h3 id="badge-rules">Un-capped Eligibility Criteria</h3>

Unlike traditional limited-seat competitions, Verified Badges are **NOT restricted** to a fixed number of winners (e.g., top 10 or top 50):

* **Merit-Driven Benchmark:** Any developer whose project passes the official rubric score threshold automatically receives an official **NGCBD Verified Badge**.
* **Equal Opportunity:** Whether 5 developers or 500 developers cross the target score in a cycle, **every single qualifying candidate** earns their badge.
* **Inspectable & Embeddable:** Badges can be embedded directly into GitHub READMEs, personal portfolios, and LinkedIn profiles as verifiable **Proof of Work**.
