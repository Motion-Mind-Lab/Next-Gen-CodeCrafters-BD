<h1 id="fair-play-overview">Fair Play & Eligibility Rules</h1>

Review the qualifying thresholds for earned credentials, re-submission policies, and strict anti-plagiarism guidelines for the bi-monthly league.

---

<h2 id="badge-thresholds">1. Threshold Marks for Verified Badges</h2>

Verified Badges are awarded purely on merit and technical achievement. They are not limited by seat quotas or cohort rankings.

<h3 id="qualification-criteria">Qualification Standard</h3>

* **Uncapped Earning Potential:** Every candidate who reaches or exceeds the required target score threshold automatically receives an official **NGCBD Verified Badge**.
* **Direct Credit Verification:** Earned credentials are tied to evaluated GitHub repositories and verified review metrics, serving as permanent proof of work.
* **Equal Standard:** Scores are calculated neutrally based on the 250-mark framework regardless of total participant count.

---

<h2 id="resubmission-rules">2. Project Eligibility & Re-Submission Guidelines</h2>

To maintain high technical standards, submissions must adhere to specific eligibility boundaries regarding codebase age and re-submission.

<h3 id="eligibility-boundaries">Submission Eligibility</h3>

* **New vs. Existing Projects:** Developers may submit existing personal projects, provided significant new features, refactoring, or architectural improvements have been committed during the active 2-month cycle.
* **Re-Submission Limits:** Projects that failed to meet the passing threshold in a previous cycle can be re-submitted in a subsequent cycle **only if** major code optimization, bug fixes, or functionality upgrades are clearly documented in the commit history.
* **Dual Project Limit:** A maximum of 2 distinct project repositories can be submitted per developer in a single cycle.

---

<h2 id="disqualification-policy">3. Disqualification & Anti-Fraud Measures</h2>

To safeguard honest developers and maintain community reputation, NGCBD enforces strict disqualification standards.

<h3 id="disqualification-triggers">Grounds for Immediate Disqualification</h3>

* **Unattributed Plagiarism:** Submitting another developer's repository or code without proper attribution in the project `README.md` or source headers.
* **Fake or Fabricated Commits:** Manipulating git commit histories or dates to fake project activity.
* **Artificial Engagement Farming:** Using bot accounts or mass cross-commenting rings to inflate community engagement marks.

For complete details on detection protocols and transparency enforcement, visit our <a href="/docs#integrity-overview">Code Integrity & Fair Play Documentation</a>.
